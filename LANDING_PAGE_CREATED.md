## 🎨 Design & UX

### Cores Utilizadas
- **Primary:** `#34c759` (Escambo Green)
- **Background:** `#ffffff` (White) / `#f9fafb` (Gray-50)
- **Text:** `#111827` (Gray-900), `#6b7280` (Gray-600)
- **Borders:** `#e5e7eb` (Gray-200)

### Tipografia
- **Headings:** Bold (700-900 weight)
- **Body:** Regular (400 weight)
- **CTA:** Semibold (600 weight)
- **Tamanhos:** Responsivos (sm:, md:, lg:)

### Animações
- ✨ Fade-in on scroll
- ✨ Stagger animations (cards)
- ✨ Bounce effect (CTAs)
- ✨ Rotating background (propósito section)
- ✨ Scroll indicator (chevron)
